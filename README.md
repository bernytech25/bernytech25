## 🧭 About Me

I'm an AI Engineer specializing in architecting production-grade agentic and RAG systems, with a Data Science foundation that shapes how I design them.

Before orchestrating multi-agent systems or retrieval pipelines, I understand the data itself: its structure, statistical behavior, and quality constraints. That is what separates my architectures from purely orchestration-focused implementations.

**My path:** Biological Sciences → Data Science → AI Engineering

- **Biological Sciences:** research rigor and evidence-based reasoning.
- **Data Science:** real projects in statistical modeling and analysis.
- **AI Engineering:** multi-cloud, observable, and tested systems built for production, not just prototypes.
